## 🌟 Project Vision

AI Karate Performance Analyzer is a 3D educational and experimental platform for studying karate as a complete human-movement system rather than simply a collection of punches and kicks.

The project connects:

> **« 🥋 Traditional Karate ↓ 🏯 Dojo Training ↓ 🏆 Tournament Karate ↓ 🧬 Sports Biomechanics ↓ 👁️ Computer Vision ↓ 🤖 Artificial Intelligence ↓ 🥽 VR / AR Training »**

The objective is to visualize, measure and analyze human movement using a 3D articulated skeleton, biomechanics, computer vision, AI and interactive simulation.

🧬 3D HUMAN DIGITAL MODEL
The central component is an articulated human skeleton. Each joint can store: Position, Orientation, Velocity, Acceleration, Joint angle, Angular velocity, Angular acceleration. The objective is to maintain physically meaningful bone relationships rather than independently moving every landmark.

📐 BIOMECHANICAL ENGINE
These quantities can be visualized beside the 3D athlete:
 * Angular velocity: ω = dθ / dt
 * Angular acceleration: α = dω / dt
 * Linear velocity: v = dx / dt
 * Linear acceleration: a = dv / dt
 * Force: F = m a
 * Torque: τ = r × F
 * Impulse: J = ∫ F dt

🎬 THE "HARP / ORCHESTRATION" IDEA
One of the creative inspirations behind the project's presentation language is the idea of martial arts being presented almost like a carefully conducted musical performance:
> «Movement → Rhythm → Timing → Coordination → Precision → Performance»
>

 * ⚡ Force = Dynamics

🎥 KUNG FU HUSTLE — CREATIVE INSPIRATION
A visual inspiration for this idea comes from the 2004 film: Kung Fu Hustle, Directed by Stephen Chow. The film famously combines martial arts, comedy, exaggerated choreography, music and theatrical presentation.
The project takes inspiration from the general artistic idea of presenting martial arts as a synchronized performance, rather than claiming that the film directly inspired the technical development of this software.

🎼 THE BLIND MUSICIANS AND THE RUDRA VEENA / RAVANA IDEA
The musical imagery also led to a fascinating cultural association with Ravana from the Ramayana tradition.
